🦙 Lethal Llamas

Objective

Teams compete to protect their terrain while reducing the opposing team’s WTF (Will to Fight) to 0. The victorious team takes over the opposing team’s terrain!

Setup

  • Players: 4–6 players in even teams (2v2 or 3v3)
  • WTF (Will to Fight):
    • 200 WTF for 2-player teams
    • 300 WTF for 3-player teams
  • Decks:
    • Action Deck (50 cards): Offensive attacks
    • Defense Deck (40 cards): Damage mitigation and counters
    • Boost Deck (30 cards): Special modifiers and effects
  • Player Hand: Each player starts with 5 cards:
    • 2 Action
    • 2 Defense
    • 1 Boost
  • Player Tokens: Track team WTF on the board

Game Flow

Rounds

  • Teams may strategize for up to 1 minute
  • Each player places one card face-down

Card Reveal

  • All cards are revealed simultaneously

Resolve Cards

  • Combine all Action cards for total attack
  • Defense cards reduce incoming damage
  • Boost cards modify attack, defense, or apply special effects
Example:
Team A: 60 Attack + 10 Boost = 70
Team B: 50 Defense
Result: Team A deals 20 damage

Card Replenishment

At the end of each round, players draw 1 card to maintain a 5-card hand.

Winning the Game

  • The game ends when a team reaches 0 WTF
  • Or after 10 rounds, the team with the lower WTF loses
  • The winning team takes the opposing terrain

Additional Rules

  • Communication: Teams may discuss strategy openly
  • Card Limit: One card per player per round
  • Random Effects: Cards are flipped face-down and the opponent chooses
  • Play First: Resolve your effects before the opposing team
  • If both teams play “Play First”:
    • Lower WTF goes first
    • If tied: coin flip, dice, or rock-paper-scissors
